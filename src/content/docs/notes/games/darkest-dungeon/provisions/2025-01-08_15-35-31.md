# Cove Provisions

## Short Run

- Food: 8-12
- Shovels: 1-2
- Anti Venoms: 0-1
- Bandages: 1-3
- Medical Herbs: 2-3
- Skeleton Keys: 1-2
- Holly Waters: 2-3
- Torches: 8-10
- **Total Cost:** 2600 - 4150

## Medium Run

- Food: 12-18
- Shovels: 2-3
- Anti Venoms: 0-2
- Bandages: 2-3
- Medical Herbs: 3-4
- Skeleton Keys: 2-3
- Holly Waters: 3
- Torches: 10-16
- **Total Cost:** 4050-8100

## Long Run

- Food: 16-20
- Shovels: 2-4
- Anti Venoms: 0-3
- Bandages: 3-4
- Medical Herbs: 3-5
- Skeleton Keys: 2-4
- Holly Waters: 3-4
- Torches: 16-20
- **Total Cost:** 4950-7700
