# Internet Protocol Address

- Identifies a device on a network but can be reassigned to another device.
- Consists of four octets, determined through IP addressing & [[subnet|subnetting]].
- Cannot be active on more than one device within the same network simultaneously.
- Govern how devices communicate over private and public networks.
- There are two types:
     - **Public IP**: Identifies a device on the Internet.
     - **Private IP**: Identifies a device within a local network.
- Provided by an ISP.
