# Point-to-Point Topology

- Is simplest network topology with a dedicated connection between two hosts.
- A direct and straightforward physical link exists only between two hosts.
- These two devices can use these connections for mutual communication.
- Point-to-point topologies are the basic model of traditional telephony.
