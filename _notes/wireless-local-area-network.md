# Wireless Local Area Network

The **Wireless Local Area Network** is an internal network that interconnects devices such as computers, laptops, printers, phones, IoT devices within a limited area such as an office, a school or your home.

The unique difference with the [[20241101182009|LAN]] is that you can transmit data without cables.
