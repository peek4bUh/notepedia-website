# Ports

- Virtual points where network connections begin and end, managed by the OS.
- Associated with specific processes/services to differentiate traffic types.
- Each port is assigned a number and many are standardized.
- Standardized port numbers can also be customized
- There are two categories:
    - [[transmission-control-protocol|TCP]]
    - [[user-datagram-protocol|UDP]]
- There are 65,535 ports for **TCP** and 65,535 ports for **UDP**.
