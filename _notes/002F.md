# Address Resolution Protocol

- Allows devices to identify themselves on a network.
- Maps IP addresses to [[20240914192253|MAC addresses]].
- Each device maintain a log of the MAC addresses associated with other devices.
- To communicate, a device broadcasts a request to find the MAC address of another device. Once found, direct communication is established.
