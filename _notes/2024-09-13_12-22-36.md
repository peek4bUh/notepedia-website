# Computer Networking

- Connects devices (computers, phones, printers, etc.) to share data and resources.
- Devices communicate using **protocols** over wired or wireless technologies.
- Networks can range from **2 devices to billions**, including laptops, phones, security cameras, and even traffic lights.
